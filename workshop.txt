title=Foraging Tooltip Extended [B42]
description=This mod enhances the default foraging tooltip by providing a much clearer and more informative breakdown of all modifiers affecting your search radius.
description=
description=**Key Features:**
description=
description=- Displays all key modifiers (weather, darkness, exhaustion, etc.) with their *actual* impact on the search radius, instead of vague percentage values.
description=- Customizable via Mod Options – choose to hide or show specific sections based on your preferences, whether you prefer a minimalistic or detailed view.
description=- Relies on in-game values for calculations rather than hardcoded logic, ensuring high compatibility with other mods (unless they modify the same tooltip directly).
description=- Helps to understand how to *actively improve* the search radius through traits, skill progression, and avoiding penalties.
description=
description=**Why this mod?**
description=
description=The vanilla tooltip provides very limited insight into what affects your foraging performance. With this mod, you’ll finally get a full picture of every factor that contributes to your vision effectiveness — making your foraging experience more rewarding.
description=
description=Compatible with and tested on Build 42.9
