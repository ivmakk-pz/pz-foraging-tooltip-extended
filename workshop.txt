title=Foraging Tooltip Extended [B42]
description=Boost your foraging experience with a detailed tooltip that breaks down all modifiers affecting your search radius.
description=
description=[h2]Key Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Detailed Modifier Breakdown:[/b] Shows exact effects of weather, darkness, exhaustion, traits, skills, and other factors on your search radius.
description=[*] [b]Persistent Radius Display:[/b] Shows current search radius next to the eye icon (optional, toggle in Mod Options).
description=[*] [b]Min/Max Indicators:[/b] Highlights when search radius reaches game limits.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Mod Options[/h2]
description=Customize under [b]Menu -> Options -> Mods -> Foraging Tooltip Extended[/b]:
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Show Radius before Eye Icon:[/b] Toggle persistent radius display (default: enabled)
description=[*] [b]Tooltip Value Alignment:[/b] Choose right or left alignment for tooltip values (default: right)
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Why I Made This[/h2]
description=I've always enjoyed foraging as a reliable source of food and medical herbs since the early days of Project Zomboid. However, Build 42's vanilla tooltip felt confusing — for example, Weather Effect appeared as both a red negative value at the top and a green negative value in the bonuses section. This dual representation made it unclear how modifiers actually interact.
description=
description=I couldn't find any working mods for Build 42 that addressed this, so I created this mod to make foraging mechanics transparent and understandable.
description=
